## Aggregation decisions

- **GLOW** — the workbook lists `Glow TB10mg+BPC-15710mg+GHK50mg` and `GLOW(BPC 157 10mg+GHK-CU50mg+TB500 10mg)` separately. Both are the same 70 mg blend (GHK-Cu 50 + BPC-157 10 + TB-500 10), so they are aggregated on one page with one 70 mg SKU.
- **KLOW** — `KLOW`, `CU50+TB10+BC10+KPV10` and `GHKCU 50+TB10+BC10+KPV10` describe the same 80 mg four-component blend. They are aggregated on one page with one 80 mg SKU.
- **BPC-157 / TB-500 blends** — the 5+5, 10+10 and 15+15 mg listings are aggregated as three pack sizes on one blend page, not three competing pages.
- **Glutathione** — `Glutathione` and `Glutathione(brand)` list the same molecule. 0.6 g and 1.5 g are normalised to 600 mg and 1500 mg; 400/600/1500 mg are variants on one page.
- **ARA-290** — `ARA-290` (40 mg) and `ARA290 (Cibinetide)` (10/16 mg) are aggregated under one CAS 1208243-50-8 page.
- **FOXO4-DRI** — `FOXO4` and `FOX04 -DRI` are aggregated; 2 mg and 10 mg are variants.
- **Thymalin** — `Thymalin` and `Thymalin/Thymulin` are aggregated as one thymic polypeptide complex page.
- **Pancragen** — the source row says `20mg*116vials`; this is a clear kit-quantity typo. The catalogue normalises it to the standard 10-vial kit and flags the batch quantity at quote stage.
- **NMN** — NMN is **not present** in the source workbook. It is included as the companion NAD+ precursor required by the brief, quoted as a bulk powder from 10 g. Confirm the final pack sizes and current batch specification before publishing prices or stock claims.
- **Matrixyl** — the workbook lists the trade name `Matrixyl`. The page supplies the equivalent palmitoyl pentapeptide-4 raw material and states that Matrixyl is a Sederma trademark. It does not claim to be the branded ingredient.

## Purity caveat

All synthetic peptides target ≥99% by RP-HPLC with ESI-MS identity, batch COA and SDS. **Thymalin is the exception**: it is a heterogeneous thymic polypeptide complex, so the page reports an HPLC profile rather than a single-peak ≥99% claim. Where a CAS number is not publicly assigned (several short bioregulator peptides and Thymalin), the page says so and relies on sequence, HPLC and MS data for identity control.

## Pricing policy

The workbook contains internal supplier/distributor cost bands. These are **not published** on the website. Public pages are quote-only: pack size, destination, documentation and quantity determine the price, and publishing internal bands would expose sourcing margins and invite stale-price disputes.
