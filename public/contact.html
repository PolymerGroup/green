Before you write

Three things that speed up a technical quote.

1. The exact molecule

Name, CAS number or sequence. For blends, state the ratio and total fill you need.

2. The destination

Country and postcode determine freight, cold-chain and customs documentation.

3. The acceptance criteria

Purity, counter-ion, water content, heavy metals or endotoxin — tell us what your QA team needs to see.
