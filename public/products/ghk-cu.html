Research overview

GHK-Cu combines a small, highly conserved tripeptide with a copper ion. The peptide sequence gives the complex its affinity for skin proteins and its signalling profile, while copper acts as a cofactor for lysyl oxidase and copper-dependent antioxidant enzymes. This dual character is why the molecule appears in both dermal-repair and antioxidant research.

As a cosmetic raw material, GHK-Cu is valued for its solubility, low use level and compatibility with emulsion systems. Vivalr supplies the copper complex — not the free tripeptide — and verifies identity, peptide purity and copper content so formulators can calculate active copper dose reliably.

Synthesis & Stability Profile

This section summarises the production route, the analytical release criteria and the degradation risks that matter when handling GHK-Cu in the laboratory or in formulation.

SPPS route & purification

Batch-released

GHK-Cu is produced by Fmoc solid-phase peptide synthesis, purified by preparative C18 HPLC to ≥99%, and then complexed with copper(II) acetate under controlled pH. The blue complex is isolated, desalted and lyophilized. Identity is confirmed by ESI-MS; copper content is verified by ICP-OES and reported on the batch COA.

Degradation risks & mitigation

pH-dependent complex stability high
The Cu(II) complex dissociates at low pH and can precipitate or exchange copper above neutral pH. Formulate at pH 4.5–6.5.
Chelator incompatibility high
EDTA, citric acid, phytate and other strong chelators strip the copper from the peptide. Keep them out of the same phase.
Redox cycling & light medium
Copper can participate in redox reactions with reducing agents such as ascorbic acid. Add at cool-down and protect from light.

Formulation & handling notes

Typical cosmetic use levels are 0.05–0.5% as supplied, depending on the target copper dose. Avoid ascorbic acid, EDTA, strong chelators and anionic surfactants in the same phase. Run a four-week accelerated compatibility test in your base before scale-up.

What are the lead time and payment terms?

Stock items usually dispatch in 2–3 business days; bulk or custom fill/finish takes 10–15 business days. We issue a proforma invoice and accept bank transfer, T/T and Wise, with terms for repeat buyers reviewed case by case.

Is GHK-Cu the same as copper tripeptide-1?

Yes. GHK-Cu, copper tripeptide-1 and the INCI name Copper Tripeptide-1 refer to the same Gly-His-Lys–copper(II) complex. Vivalr supplies the blue complex, not the uncomplexed tripeptide, and reports copper content on the COA.

Can GHK-Cu be combined with vitamin C?

Not in the same phase. Ascorbic acid is a reducing agent and can destabilise the copper complex and discolour the formula. If a vitamin C step is required, separate the phases, use a non-reducing derivative, and validate colour and pH over four weeks.
