Research overview

Pinealon's charge profile — two acidic residues and a basic arginine — makes it highly water-soluble and gives it a distinct interaction profile at the cell membrane. It is one of the most researched short bioregulators in neuro-aging models.

Vivalr supplies Pinealon as a ≥99% HPLC-purified lyophilized powder with MS identity data.

Research focus

Published work on Pinealon spans several complementary lines of investigation. The summaries below describe research areas, not therapeutic claims or recommended uses.

Neuroprotection

Studied for neuronal survival under oxidative and excitotoxic stress.

Brain-aging models

Investigated for cognitive and neurotrophic signalling in aging models.

Oxidative stress

Evaluated for antioxidant and mitochondrial effects in neural cells.

Bioregulator research

A reference tripeptide in the Khavinson peptide family.

Synthesis & Stability Profile

This section summarises the production route, the analytical release criteria and the degradation risks that matter when handling Pinealon in the laboratory or in formulation.

SPPS route & purification

Batch-released

Pinealon is produced by Fmoc SPPS, cleaved and purified by preparative C18 HPLC, then converted to the acetate salt and lyophilized.

Degradation risks & mitigation

Arginine side-chain degradation low
Protect from strong alkali and prolonged heat; arginine-containing peptides can degrade at high pH.
Hygroscopicity medium
Store with desiccant and equilibrate before weighing.
Peptide hydrolysis low
Use pH 5–7 and avoid autoclaving.

What is the sequence of Pinealon?

Pinealon is Glu-Asp-Arg (EDR), CAS 175175-23-2, with a calculated molecular weight of 418.40 Da.
