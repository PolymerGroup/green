At a glance · quotable summary

BPC-157 / TB-500 Blend is a fixed-ratio research blend supplied by Vivalr (Shandong Green International Trade Co., Ltd.): BPC-157 + TB-500, 1:1 ratio. It is prepared from independently synthesized and purified components and ships with a component-level COA and MS data. Listed from $124.73 per kit; MOQ 1 kit (10 vials).

Research overview

BPC-157 and TB-500 act on different parts of the repair cascade: BPC-157 is associated with angiogenic and gastrointestinal signalling, while TB-500 is linked to actin dynamics and cell migration. Researchers combine them to study complementary repair pathways.

Each component is synthesized and purified separately before being combined. This matters for documentation: the COA reports each peptide's purity and identity, not just a single blend value.

Synthesis & Stability Profile

This section summarises the production route, the analytical release criteria and the degradation risks that matter when handling BPC-157 / TB-500 Blend in the laboratory or in formulation.

SPPS route & purification

Batch-released

BPC-157 and TB-500 are synthesized and HPLC-purified independently. The purified peptides are quantified, combined in a 1:1 mass ratio, sterile-filtered and co-lyophilized. Each component is identity-tested before blending.

Degradation risks & mitigation

Component-specific oxidation medium
TB-500 contains methionine and is the more oxidation-sensitive component; protect the blend from light and oxygen.
Aggregation medium
The large TB-500 peptide can aggregate; keep working solutions dilute and avoid foaming.
Content uniformity medium
Co-lyophilized blends require validated mixing; request the blend uniformity data for your batch.
