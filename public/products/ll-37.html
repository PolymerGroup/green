Research overview

LL-37's cationic, amphipathic structure lets it interact with microbial membranes and host cells. That dual activity makes it a model peptide for innate-immunity and tissue-repair research, but also a challenging molecule to formulate.

Vivalr supplies synthetic LL-37 as a ≥99% HPLC-purified lyophilized powder with MS identity.

Research focus

Published work on LL-37 spans several complementary lines of investigation. The summaries below describe research areas, not therapeutic claims or recommended uses.

Antimicrobial activity

Studied against bacteria, fungi and biofilm-forming organisms.

Innate immunity

Investigated for chemotaxis, cytokine modulation and host-defence signalling.

Wound repair

Evaluated for keratinocyte migration, angiogenesis and re-epithelialization.

Biofilm disruption

Studied for membrane disruption and biofilm inhibition.

Synthesis & Stability Profile

This section summarises the production route, the analytical release criteria and the degradation risks that matter when handling LL-37 in the laboratory or in formulation.

SPPS route & purification

Batch-released

LL-37 is produced by Fmoc SPPS, cleaved and purified by preparative C18 HPLC. The acetate salt is lyophilized; identity is confirmed by ESI-MS.

Degradation risks & mitigation

Aggregation & membrane activity high
The amphipathic peptide self-assembles and can adsorb to surfaces. Use low-binding tubes and avoid foaming.
Proteolysis medium
Susceptible to proteases; handle aseptically and store working solutions cold.
pH-dependent charge medium
Activity and solubility depend on pH; use a defined buffer and validate.
