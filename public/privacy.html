Privacy policy

How Vivalr handles the information you send when you request a quote or browse this website. Effective 27 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Shandong Green International Trade Co., Ltd. (“Vivalr”, “we”, “us”), located at Environmental Protection Science and Technology Park, High-Tech Zone, Jinan City, Shandong Province, China. For privacy questions, contact info@vivalr.com.

What we collect

We only receive personal information that you choose to send us. That normally includes your name, company, email address, country, product interest, quantity and any technical requirements you include in an email, WhatsApp message or the RFQ form.

The RFQ form on this site does not submit to a server. It composes an email or WhatsApp message in your own application, so the information reaches us only when you press send there.

Website analytics and cookies

This is a static website. We do not set advertising cookies, run third-party analytics, or build behavioural profiles. Cloudflare, our hosting provider, processes standard technical logs (IP address, request path, user agent, timestamp) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Those logs are governed by Cloudflare's own privacy terms.

How we use your information

  • To prepare and send a quotation, COA or technical response.
  • To arrange payment, shipping and customs documentation if you place an order.
  • To keep records required for export, tax and quality purposes.
  • To answer follow-up technical or commercial questions.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary with service providers such as couriers, banks, customs brokers and — where you request third-party testing — the nominated laboratory. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

International transfers

Our team is based in China and works with partners and carriers worldwide. If you are in the EEA or UK, your information may be transferred outside your region. We take reasonable steps to ensure that recipients handle it under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection terms.

Retention

Quotation correspondence is kept for as long as needed to support the commercial relationship and applicable record-keeping requirements. You can ask us to delete inquiry data that is not required for an active order or a legal obligation.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, and to request a portable copy. Contact info@vivalr.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and limited retention to protect inquiry data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure; please avoid sending sensitive personal or medical information through the RFQ channels.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our operations or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of the page will change when we do.
