Home/About

A Shandong-based supplier built for research buyers.

Vivalr is the research-peptide brand of Shandong Green International Trade Co., Ltd.. Based in Shandong, China, we supply cosmetic actives, repair and bioregulator peptides, fixed-ratio blends, and NAD+/NMN raw materials to laboratories, formulators, and ingredient buyers worldwide.

Who we are

More than an export trader — a contract manufacturing partner.

We are not just an export-focused trading company. We are also a contract manufacturer. That distinction matters: we translate your specification into a qualified synthesis and fill-finish batch, verify the documentation, and get it to your laboratory without surprises.

Our contract manufacturing team is based in Shandong, one of China's established chemical and biotechnology export clusters. We manage synthesis, purification, and lyophilization through a qualified production network, and we release every lot against a written specification before it is offered to a buyer.

Our listed prices cover standard pack sizes in USD. Final pricing depends on pack size, counter-ion, documentation, and destination, and we confirm every detail in a written quotation before production.

What we optimise for

Three commitments we can actually control.

Documentation first

Every batch is released with a COA, HPLC chromatogram, MS spectrum and SDS. If a number is not on the certificate, ask — we would rather provide the data than defend a claim.

Research-use discipline

We supply research material only. No dosage guidance, no medical claims, no finished retail products. Buyers receive a clear research-use statement with every order.

Low-MOQ access

Start with one 10-vial kit and scale to 100+ kits on the same specification. Custom fill, labelling and kit assembly are available from modest minimums.

How we work

No black boxes between the quote and the vial.

A named contact, a written specification and a dispatch date. That is the operating model.

01

Technical intake

We confirm the molecule, counter-ion, pack size, destination and any analytical requirements before quoting.

02

Written quotation

Price, specification, lead time, shipping method and documentation are stated in one document.

03

Batch release

The lot is tested and the COA is reviewed against the agreed specification before packing.

04

Dispatch & aftercare

Tracking, packing photos and documentation are sent. We stay available for technical follow-up.

Compliance position. Vivalr supplies raw materials for laboratory research and formulation evaluation only. We do not manufacture, market or sell finished pharmaceutical, cosmetic or veterinary products, and we do not provide medical or dosage advice. Buyers are responsible for ensuring that their use complies with the regulations of their jurisdiction.
